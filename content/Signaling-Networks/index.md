# Signaling & Networks

The tracks are the veins of your empire, but bad signaling is a blood clot. This silo is dedicated to keeping the flow constant.

## Common Pain Points
* **The Head-to-Head Deadlock:** "Why are my trains staring at each other?"
* **Path vs. Block:** When to use standard signals vs. the modern Path Signal.
* **Station Throughput:** Designing RO-RO (Roll-In, Roll-Out) stations to prevent mainline jams.
