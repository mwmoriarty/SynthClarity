# Cinematic Echo

The secrets of the Krell did not die with their civilization. This topic maps the lineage of *Forbidden Planet* across modern sci-fi, progressive rock, and contemporary sound design.

## Current Research:
* **[[Star Trek DNA]]:** Tracing the direct links from Commander Adams to Captain Kirk.
* **[[Rush Connection]]:** Exploring the influence of the Krell aesthetic on Prog-Rock.
* **[[Sonic Successors]]:** From Ben Burtt to the birth of modern cinematic sound.
