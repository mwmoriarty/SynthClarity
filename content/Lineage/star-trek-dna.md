# Federation Blueprint

Gene Roddenberry famously called *Forbidden Planet* one of his primary inspirations. The DNA of the United Planets Starship C-57D is visible in every deck of the USS Enterprise.

## Command Structure
Commander John J. Adams, Doc Ostrow, and Lt. Farman established the "Triumvirate" dynamic:
1. **The Captain (Adams/Kirk):** The decisive military leader.
2. **The Scientist (Farman/Spock):** The logical, technical mind.
3. **The Medical Officer (Ostrow/McCoy):** The humanist and moral compass.

## Visual Cues
From the **handheld communicators** and **blaster pistols** to the concept of a **unified Earth military** exploring the stars, the lineage is direct and undeniable.
