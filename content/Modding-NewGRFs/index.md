# Modding & NewGRFs

OpenTTD is defined by its mods, but "mod it until it breaks" is a real risk. This silo focuses on building a stable, complex, and rewarding NewGRF load order.

> [!warning] Compatibility First
> Adding or removing NewGRFs from an **existing** save game can permanently corrupt your world. Always test your "Load Order" in a new game before committing to a long-term play.
