Hi, I’m Michael!

I am a **_Front-End Developer_**, content creator, and educator; passionate about empowering others to thrive in the digital world.

I graduated from **_Electronics Engineering Technology_** at [[https://www.fanshawe.ca |Fanshawe College]] in London, ON, Canada, in 1996 and earned my certification as an **_Applied Science Technologist_** from [[http://oacett.org |OACETT]] shortly thereafter.

One of my college instructors often said, **_“Never implement in hardware what you can implement in software.”_** It is especially relevant as we navigate an era where software powers innovation in automation, connectivity, and **_digital solutions_**.

Over the years, I have discovered that my true passion lies in **_web design and digital empowerment_**. Designing responsive, dynamic websites and crafting compelling online content allows me to blend creativity with technical expertise.

Beyond the joy of creating, I have found purpose in helping others. If I can illuminate others with **_better strategies and techniques_** that is reward in itself. Helping others overcome challenges, harness their potential, and achieve their goals is the ultimate.

Thank you for visiting my website! Whether you are looking for tips on how to play [[https://github.com/openttd|OpenTTD]] better, or have a hurdle you cannot seem to overcome, my case studies in [[common-hurdles | Common Hurdles]] and the [[master-notes | Mastery Notes]] man help.

My hope is that you will find something valuable here. Feel free to reach out with any questions or suggestions — I am always happy to [[contact | connect]]!
