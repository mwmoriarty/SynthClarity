Hi, I’m Michael!

I am a [Front-End Developer](https://www.ttdclarity.com/front-end-developer), content creator, and educator passionate about empowering others to thrive in the digital world. I graduated from the _Electronics Engineering Technology_ Cooperative program at [Fanshawe College](https://www.fanshawe.ca/) in London, ON, Canada, in 1996 and earned my certification as an _Applied Science Technologist_ from [O.A.C.E.T.T.](http://oacett.org/ shortly after.

One of my college instructors often said, _“Never implement in hardware what you can implement in software.”_. That advice has stuck with me throughout my career and remains especially relevant as we navigate an era where software powers innovation in automation, connectivity, and _digital solutions_.

Over the years, I have discovered that my true passion lies in _web design and digital empowerment_. Designing responsive, dynamic websites and crafting compelling online content allows me to blend creativity with technical expertise. Beyond the joy of creating, I have found purpose in helping others—teaching web design and online strategies to budding entrepreneurs, so they can overcome challenges, harness their potential, and achieve their goals.

I also dedicate time to addressing the “[_digital divide_](https://www.digital-divide.ca/)” — a generational gap in technology access and understanding. Through hands-on workshops, one-on-one lessons, and peer-led drop-in sessions, I have helped older adults embrace technology with confidence and enthusiasm.

Thank you for visiting my website! Whether you’re looking for tips to _establish your online presence_, guidance on navigating digital challenges, or simply inspiration, I hope you will find something valuable here. Feel free to reach out with any questions or suggestions — I am always happy to [connect](https://www.ttdclarity.com/contact).
